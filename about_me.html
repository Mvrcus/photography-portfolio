About Zuri Visuals

zuri

Holland-based senior & lifestyle photographer

Zuri Visuals focuses on high school seniors and graduation portraits across Holland, Zeeland, Grand Haven, and the West Michigan lakeshore. Sessions emphasize natural light, real personality, and locations you actually care about—Lake Michigan beaches, downtown brick, dunes, and the hobbies that define your senior year.

Beyond senior work, the portfolio includes outdoor lifestyle, pets, and milestone portraits that share the same documentary-meets-editorial approach: guided but never stiff, with room for laughter, movement, and the messy-beautiful details of being seventeen or eighteen on the lakeshore.

New to senior photos? Start with our free senior picture guides—locations, outfits, timing, and pricing—or jump straight to session details.
