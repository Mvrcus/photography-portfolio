Senior Portraits & Graduation Photos

Sessions for Holland, Zeeland, Grand Haven, and West Michigan seniors who want photos that look like them, not a generic studio package.

What's included

  • 60–90 minute outdoor session at a location you love (beach, downtown, dunes, or campus-adjacent spots)
  • Multiple outfits and looks — casual, dressy, sports, hobbies, or pets
  • Editing that keeps natural color and skin tones
  • Online gallery for sharing with family and social media
  • Help picking what to wear and where to shoot

Who it's for

Class of 2026 (and 2027+) students at Holland, West Ottawa, Zeeland East/West, Hamilton, Black River, Grand Haven, Spring Lake, and nearby schools. Yearbook headshots, announcement photos, or senior pictures you actually want to post. We'll plan around what you need.
