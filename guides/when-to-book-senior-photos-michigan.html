When to Book Senior Photos in Michigan

Timeline for class of 2026 · Holland & West Michigan schools

Michigan seniors—and parents searching when to take senior pictures—often wait until summer and find prime weekend slots gone. Booking early secures golden-hour Lake Michigan dates and gives time for hair, outfits, and announcement ordering. Below is a practical timeline used by many West Michigan studios and aligned with general senior-session guidance from industry groups like PPA.

Junior year spring (April–June)

  • Best for: Early planners, athletes with busy senior fall, tulip season in Holland
  • Pros: Less competition for dates; unique seasonal backdrops
  • Cons: Some students prefer a more "senior" look closer to graduation

Summer before senior year (June–August)

  • Best for: Lake Michigan beach sessions, travel-free schedules, class of 2026 main gallery
  • Pros: Warm weather, sunset times, variety of locations (see locations guide)
  • Cons: Peak demand—book 2–4 months ahead for July and August

Senior fall (September–October)

  • Best for: Fall color, cozy layers, yearbook submission if deadline allows
  • Watch: School, sports, and college application crunch; confirm your yearbook cutoff with counseling office

Senior spring (March–May)

  • Best for: Cap-and-gown, second outfit round, "we missed summer" catch-up sessions
  • Cons: Cooler beach weather; tulip crowds in Holland in May

Yearbook & announcement deadlines

Every district differs—Holland, West Ottawa, Zeeland, and Grand Haven schools often collect senior portraits in early fall of senior year. Allow 2–4 weeks after your session for gallery delivery and cropping. If you're targeting graduation announcements, order proofs soon after your summer shoot so print labs meet mail dates.

How far in advance to contact your photographer

For West Michigan summer weekends: 3–4 months. Shoulder seasons (May, September): 6–8 weeks. Last-minute openings happen—ask—but don't rely on them for your only outfit at Holland State Park on a Saturday in July.
